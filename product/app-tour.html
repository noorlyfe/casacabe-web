Dashboard

Revenue, expenses, net profit, gross profit, prime cost %, cost ratios, revenue by channel, recent activity, and unpaid invoices. All update as you enter data.

Sales

Log sales by channel (Dine-In, Takeout, Delivery, Catering, Bar, Other) with a monthly chart and year-to-date totals.

Expenses

Log expenses by category (COGS, labor, operations, occupancy) with a cost group breakdown and food & beverage as a share of sales.

Invoices

Invoice builder with line items, sales tax, live preview, PDF export, and payment status tracking.

Import

CSV import for sales and expenses with downloadable templates. Optional folder watch for auto-import.

Products

Ingredient and supply catalog with units and price history. Used for food cost, purchasing, and price alerts.

Vendors

Supplier directory with contact info, payment terms, linked products, and invoice matching by name.

Purchasing

Purchase orders from draft to sent to received. Prefill from a vendor order guide and optionally create an invoice on receipt.

Inventory

Count sheets, draft inventory counts, waste entries, and inventory value tracking with a usage report between counts.

Reports

Full P&L statement with revenue breakdown, COGS, labor, prime cost, net profit, net margin, and industry benchmarks. Export to CSV and PDF.

Budgets

Per-category budget rules as a fixed amount or % of sales. Variance vs actual, plus a prime cost target panel.

Price Alerts

Manual price monitoring from your product catalog. Set a threshold % and get alerted when a recorded price rises above it. Not automatic. Based on prices you log yourself.

Calendar

Accounting year overview by month and quarter. Export full year data. Close the year after December 31.

Staff

Employee roster with roles, hourly or salary pay type, hours per week, and weekly and monthly labor cost projection. Used for prime cost tracking. Not payroll.

Settings

Restaurant name, owner, address, currency, prime cost target, accounting year, export, backup, and restore. Settings lives at the bottom of the sidebar.
