How Casacabe works

Three steps and you are running your restaurant numbers from one desktop dashboard on your Mac or PC. No cloud lock in. No surprise bills.

  1. 01

    Buy once

    Checkout takes about two minutes through Stripe. You get Mac and Windows installers plus your license key by email. No account on our servers. No waiting for approval.

  2. 02

    Install on your computer

    Run it like any desktop app. Your numbers live in a local database on your machine. Export anytime. Delete anytime. Back it up however you want.

  3. 03

    See the numbers

    Enter sales, expenses, staff hours, and vendor invoices. Casacabe shows prime cost, margins, and alerts. Built for owners who do their own admin.

A typical week

Most owners spend 15 to 30 minutes keeping Casacabe up to date.

Monday

Log last week’s sales from your POS report.

Midweek

Add vendor invoices as they arrive. Mark bills paid when you send payment.

Friday

Check prime cost and labor percentage before the weekend rush.

Month end

Export a Profit and Loss report to CSV or PDF for your accountant.

Where the numbers come from

Casacabe v1 does not sync with Toast, Square, or QuickBooks. You enter or import your own numbers. That keeps the app simple and under your control. Many owners pull POS totals weekly and enter them in under five minutes.

If you need live POS feeds or automatic bank sync, this is not the right fit today. We say that here so you are not surprised after purchase.

Private by default

Nothing is uploaded to our servers. We cannot see your revenue, wages, or invoices. There is no cloud toggle because there is no cloud. Your data is as private as the files on your hard drive.
