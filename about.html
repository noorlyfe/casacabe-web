About

Built for the owner at the kitchen table

Casacabe is for the independent restaurant owner who runs one location and does their own books at the kitchen table after close, or at the office desk between services.

Why we built it

Most restaurant software is built for chains, accountants, or companies that want a monthly fee forever. Independent owners end up with Toast for sales, QuickBooks for accounting, and spreadsheets in between. None of them talk to each other. The owner becomes the glue.

Casacabe is one app on your computer for the numbers you already track manually. Revenue, labor, expenses, invoices, and prime cost in one dashboard, without another subscription.

Pay once

The app and all updates through v1. You pay once and own the license on your computer. No monthly bill. The number is on pricing.

Local first

Your restaurant data lives on your Mac or PC. We do not host your books. We cannot see your revenue, wages, or invoices.

No account on our servers. No sync toggle. You install the app, you own the data, you back it up however you want.

Your data. Local only.

This is for you if

  • You run an independent restaurant with one location
  • You do your own admin and backoffice
  • You want to know if the month was profitable before your accountant tells you
  • You are tired of paying monthly subscriptions for tools that do not connect
  • You want your financial data on your computer, not in someone else’s cloud
  • You track prime cost and labor percentage manually today

This is not for you if

  • You run a chain or multiple locations
  • You need live POS sync with Toast, Square, or similar
  • You need built-in payroll tax filing or direct deposit
  • You need a full accountant-grade general ledger
  • You need recipe costing
  • You want a cloud app you can open from any device

Honest limits

We tell you what Casacabe does not do before you buy. No coming soon integrations that never ship. No copy that implies live Toast sync when there is none.

Respect the owner

No dark patterns. No surprise price hikes on v1. No chat bots pretending to be humans. Real email support from a real person.

Simple on purpose

One job: help one independent owner see their restaurant numbers clearly. Not an ERP. Not a payroll processor. Not a POS.

Support

Questions before or after purchase: support@casacabe.com. We answer by email, usually within one business day. No phone trees. No ticket systems that go nowhere.
